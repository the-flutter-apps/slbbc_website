Drop your images here:

  hero.jpg   — The cover/banner photo shown on the Home page hero section
               (the industrial site photo you shared)
               Recommended: 1200×900px, JPG, landscape

  logo.png   — The SLB circular logo (optional — the site uses an inline SVG
               logo that already matches your brand mark)
               If you provide this, update components/shared/Logo.tsx to use
               next/image pointing to /images/logo.png

Both filenames are case-sensitive. The hero image will automatically fall back
to an Unsplash industrial photo if hero.jpg is not present.
